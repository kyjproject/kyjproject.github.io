Quick start

Four steps from opening the app to your first spaced-repetition review.

1. Browse the bank
Search or filter by category, skill, difficulty, and status in Browse, then click any question to expand its full detail view — choices, rationale for every option, and the chart/table image if it has one.
2. Practice a scope
Pick a category, skill, or difficulty (optionally shuffled) in Practice and work through it one question at a time. Picking a choice just selects it — hit Submit to see if it was right, or Show answer any time you want the key and rationale without submitting.
3. Mark as you go
From any question's detail view — Browse, Practice, Test results, wherever — mark it 🚩 to review, ✓ complete, or ○ incomplete. This is what feeds the Analysis tab, the Review queue, and weak-skill targeting everywhere else.
4. Study your Review queue
The Review tab schedules everything you flagged with real spaced repetition (FSRS, the same model Anki uses). Hit Study due and it only ever shows you what's actually due — however big the backlog gets — then grade yourself Again/Hard/Good/Easy to reschedule it.

Study modes

Four ways to work through questions, roughly lowest to highest stakes.

Test
Configure categories/skills/difficulty/count and a status scope (e.g. only "Incomplete"), then take a no-feedback quiz — choices are just marked selected, nothing is graded until you submit the whole thing — followed by a scored results page with a per-skill breakdown.
Mock Exam
A fully configurable digital-SAT-style R&W mock. Pick adaptive full exam (Module 2 gets harder or easier based on your Module 1 accuracy), or drill Module 1 or Module 2 alone. Every per-skill question count and difficulty mix is editable, with an optional per-question timer on top of the real 32-minute clock. A full adaptive run scores an estimated composite /1600; module-only runs get a raw breakdown. Past attempts save locally and stay reviewable.
Bluebook-style interface
An opt-in checkbox on the Test and Mock Exam setup screens, not a separate mode — turn it on and that attempt runs full-screen, built to look and behave like the real College Board testing app: two-pane passage/question layout, Mark for Review flags, a Highlights & Notes pen, an Answer Eliminator cross-out toggle, text zoom, and a question navigator grid. Leave it off and you get the regular Test/Mock layout instead — which now has its own lightweight Mark for review toggle and Jump to question navigator too, so you can flag and revisit questions either way.
Review — spaced repetition
Everything flagged 🚩 gets scheduled by an FSRS engine — Study due for the graded flow above, or Practice all flagged for a plain no-grading run-through of the whole flagged set regardless of what's due today.

Cool features worth knowing about

Easy to miss on a first pass — worth a deliberate look.

Weak-skill bias
Question selection in Test, Practice, and Mock Exam can weight toward the skills you're actually missing — combining recent accuracy, how long a skill's gone untouched, and skills you've been quietly avoiding. Look for the toggle in each mode's setup screen.
Tags & Notes
Add free-form labels or a note to any question from its detail view. The Notes tab compiles every note across the whole bank into one searchable list, so your own commentary is never buried inside a single question again.
Analysis (under Profile)
An overall completion bar plus a per-category breakdown — click a category for a clickable tile grid, one square per question, colored by status. The Focus selector scopes all of it to just Easy/Medium/Hard, e.g. to track "Hard only" completion. Below that: a skill-mastery table with an improving/backsliding trend signal, a category × difficulty grid, and a missed-question digest pulled straight from your mock-exam history — the fastest way to see what's actually going wrong.
Gamification
XP, levels, streaks, a daily goal, and achievements, all on the Profile tab — derived automatically from progress you're already logging, nothing to track twice.
Command palette⌘K / Ctrl+K
One search box over everything from anywhere in the app — jump to a tab, jump straight to a question by its ID, or toggle theme/timer, without touching the mouse.
Export / Import / kyj-cloud
All progress lives in this browser's storage by default — use Export progress (.json) on your Profile tab to back it up and Import progress to restore or move it to another device. Optionally, sign up for kyj-cloud (the cloud icon in the topbar, or the card on your Profile tab) to sync the same progress across devices instead — a username + password, nothing syncs automatically, so press Save progress now whenever you want this device's progress pushed up.
Dark theme
The sun/moon toggle in the topbar — or flip it from the command palette.

Found a bug or have an idea? Use the feedback icon (topbar, next to the theme toggle) — or see everything that's shipped so far, in the order it was built, in What's new below.
